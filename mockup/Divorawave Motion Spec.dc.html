GUARDS — NON-NEGOTIABLE
  • 3 Hz floor: no element may flash faster than 3 Hz under any circumstance. Ambience is smoothed (release ~300ms); playhead tops out at 1.3 Hz; accents are velocity, not strobe.
  • Reduced motion is first-class: honor prefers-reduced-motion and mirror it with the in-app MOTION toggle. Static sunset, crossfade-only transitions, --pulse locked to 0.
  • GPU discipline: animate transform and opacity only; the grid is a composited plane (rotateX wrapper, translateY child). No layout-affecting animation anywhere.
  • Idle is calm: the grid drifts; nothing else moves until the user or the music does.
