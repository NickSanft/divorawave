CONCEPT

Ideas sprung from the ether. A perpetual neon sunset over a slow perspective grid; chord suggestions condense out of the horizon haze as glowing orbs — each one a small sun, scaled and brightened by its score. All boldness is spent on the Ether field. The Deck and control bar stay quiet and disciplined: dark glass, restrained neon, mono data. The site sounds like it looks — vaporwave visuals, synthwave harmony.
